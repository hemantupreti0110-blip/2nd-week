Slow work, made on foot

Mira Voss studies horizons — the specific, shifting line where one kind of land gives way to sky. Each print starts as a week or more spent in one place: walking the same ridge at different hours, waiting for weather, returning the next year to see what changed.

The result is less reportage than record: a set of studies that ask what a place looked like on one particular morning, and whether it still does.
